How it works

01

Investigate

Separate benign signals, actionable findings, and uncertainty. Signals are classified and routed: dismiss, investigate, or remediate. Ambiguous cases go to human review.

02

Verify

The proposed change is installed in an isolated environment and tested. A failure here blocks progress — the model cannot override deterministic QA.

03

Approve and release

Review the exact artifact — dependency diff, test results, source citations, and remaining uncertainty — before a human decision gates local staging.

Evidence before approval

What changed?

A reviewable dependency diff — original and proposed requirements, unified diff, SHA-256 bound. No hidden changes.

What passed?

Installed-version checks and regression results from an isolated test run. JUnit-structured per-test outcomes, not text heuristics.

What remains uncertain?

Explicit limits and human handoff. Exploitability is unverified. The historical advisory target is not a current-security guarantee.

Review the evidence. Make the call.
